How estimates work

Create a container with its starting food weight, then occasionally enter how many grams remain. Consumption between two measurements is spread uniformly over the elapsed time between them. Daily, weekly, monthly, and graph totals include those estimated grams automatically.

No consumption is extrapolated beyond the newest measurement. For your own sake, don't insert a measurement that is heavier than a previous one.
