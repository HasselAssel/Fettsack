How estimates work

A tracked container can contain multiple saved foods. Its initial tracked weight is the sum of all ingredient starting weights. Between two checkpoints, the measured weight loss is spread uniformly over time and across ingredients according to their starting proportions.

No intake is extrapolated after the newest checkpoint. Ingredient composition is locked after the first checkpoint because ingredients do not have their own timestamps.
